# Examples of Industry Terminology (MC-02-03 §4)

Reference for how common words and industry terms should be written: one word vs. two words vs.
hyphenated, and letter casing. "Notes" clarifies when hyphenation/casing depends on usage (e.g., noun
vs. adjective vs. verb). Not included here: acronyms (see `acronyms-glossary.md`) or names of
pins/bits/modes/commands (see `units-and-conventions.md` §6.5).
