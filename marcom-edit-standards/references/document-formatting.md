# Document Formatting Guidelines (MC-02-03 §3)

Applies to documents laid out in tools like Adobe InDesign that become PDFs or printed material —
not to web pages or dynamically-reflowing content. FrameMaker/Tridion technical documentation
should follow that tool's own established defaults where they differ from these instructions.

## Columns
- Balance column lengths on a page to avoid unnecessary white space.

## Line and Column Breaks
- Avoid a hyphenated word at: the first line of a paragraph/column, the last full line of a
  paragraph/column, or the last line of a page.
- Never let a digit/number be separated from its unit of measurement by a line or column break
  (e.g., "20" and "MHz" must stay together on the same line).

## Orphans and Widows
- Eliminate both:
  - **Orphan** — the last line of a paragraph sitting alone at the top of a page/column.
  - **Widow** — a single word sitting alone on the last line of a paragraph.
